Start LayerLoom

Mac early access · 0.6.4a6

The app includes everything it needs. No Python, Git, Homebrew, or Terminal setup.

1. Copy and open

  1. Drag LayerLoom.app onto Applications in the disk image.
  2. Open Applications → LayerLoom. Allow about half a minute on first launch.
Mac blocked the app?

Dismiss the warning. Open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, find LayerLoom and click Open Anyway. Confirm and enter your Mac password if asked. If the button is absent, try opening the copied app again, then return to Settings.

This free build is not Apple-notarized. Approve only the copy from the LayerLoom repository. Managed Macs may prohibit approval. If macOS says damaged or will damage your computer, stop and report the exact warning.

2. Try the example

  1. Click Try the example. Four colored cubes will appear.
  2. Click Export geometry 3MF…. Keep Woven apparent colors and 0.16 mm, then click Choose save location….
  3. Choose a filename and folder. Wait for Export complete, then click Show in Finder. The first export may take a little longer.

You have made a printable model. No printer is needed to try this example. You can eject the installer after copying; the installed app and examples work offline.

What next?

A 3MF is a printable model file. A slicer turns it into instructions for your printer. Follow the slicer walkthrough before printing.

Try painting or a larger model

Turn on Paint, select a surface, choose a color, then Apply paint. Use Save paint… to keep an editable project. Export a separate printable 3MF. Turn Paint off to return to Arrange; your edits stay.

Choose Help → Load the Benchy example to try a larger model.

If something goes wrong

Use Help → Copy diagnostics and Help → Open logs. Include the exact message and step that failed. Report a problem.

Apple Silicon needs macOS 14 or newer; Intel needs macOS 15 or newer. Online installation guide.
