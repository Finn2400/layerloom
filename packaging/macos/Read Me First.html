Start LayerLoom

  1. Drag LayerLoom.app onto the Applications folder in this disk image.
  2. Open Applications in Finder, then double-click LayerLoom.
  3. If macOS blocks the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security, click Open Anyway beside LayerLoom, then approve the confirmation. Only approve the copy you downloaded from the official LayerLoom release.
  4. Click Try the example. After the cubes appear, click Weave and choose a folder.
  5. Use Show in Finder to find the finished 3MF. Open it in your slicer, keep the parts aligned, and use 0.16 mm for both first and later layers with adaptive layers disabled.
Security approval is a graphical step.

This free build has an ad-hoc signature, without Apple notarization. Approval may be required again after an update. Organization-managed Macs may prohibit approval. If Open Anyway is unavailable, contact the computer's administrator.

No Python, Terminal, or internet connection is required to run the installed app.
